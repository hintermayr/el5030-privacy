# Privacy Policy

**App:** EL-5030 Scientific Calculator
**Package name:** de.hintermayr.sharprechner
**Last updated:** 7 October 2026

## Summary

This app collects, stores or transmits **no personal data**. There are no ads, no tracking and no internet access.

## What data is processed?

The app only processes the numbers and formulas that you enter into the calculator yourself. This data is:

- stored **locally on your device only** (via the Android "SharedPreferences" feature), so that your memory values and AER formulas are preserved after you close the app,
- **never transmitted** to us or to any third party,
- completely removed as soon as you uninstall the app or clear its data from the Android settings.

## Permissions

The app requests **no special permissions**. In particular, it does not need internet access, location, camera, contacts, or storage access outside its own app area.

## No sharing with third parties

Because no data is collected, no data is shared with or sold to third parties. No analytics or advertising libraries are used.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect any data from users, regardless of age.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when necessary. The current version is always available at its published address.
